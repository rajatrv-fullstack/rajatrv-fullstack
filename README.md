### What I actually did

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cryptography at national scale** | Co-designed the two-layer scheme behind Login.gov with the GSA CISO. Their August 2026 roadmap reports 180M+ accounts, 500M+ sign-ins a year, 700+ live sites and 54 agencies. [Roadmap PDF](https://login.gov/docs/login-gov-roadmap-august-2026.pdf) |
| **Federal cloud precedent** | Led the TIC 2.0 Overlay pilot at 18F with DHS, Sandia and the FedRAMP office. It passed. FedRAMP authorizations followed for Login.gov, cloud.gov and vote.gov. |
| **Adopted by the vendor** | AWS built my WAF geographic filtering into the product. [The release](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2017/10/aws-waf-now-supports-geographic-match/) · [The original post](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/how-to-use-aws-waf-to-filter-incoming-traffic-from-embargoed-countries/) |
| **Audits with nothing in them** | ISO 27001:2022 first attempt, zero nonconformities, UKAS accredited. 2024 and 2025 cycles across HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA and LGPD all closed "None Noted". |
| **AI in production, under audit** | A fifteen-agent security platform, autonomous triage with per-run SOC 2 reasoning logged, and ISO 42001 activated ahead of demand. An honest self-assessment put 89 control gaps in front of the CEO. |
| **I still ship** | [CertScore](https://certscore.org) and [Honor Library](https://honorlibrary.org), live on both app stores. Named inventor on a pending U.S. patent. |
